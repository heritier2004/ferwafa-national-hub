# ⚡ AI Pitch Machine (Universal Platform)

Welcome to the **FERWAFA National Football Intelligence** Edge Client. This software is designed to run locally at your stadium/academy to process live camera feeds using AI.

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Install Python**: Ensure you have Python 3.10 or newer installed ([python.org](https://www.python.org)).
2. **Download & Extract**: Unzip the `ai_machine_universal.zip` to a folder on your computer.
3. **Launch**:
   - **Windows**: Double-click `start_windows.bat`.
   - **Mac/Linux**: Open a terminal in the folder and run `bash start_mac_linux.sh`.

## ⚙️ First-Time Setup
On the first launch, the **Setup Wizard** will appear. You will need:
- Your **National Hub API Key** (provided by FERWAFA).
- The **Match Token** for the current fixture.
- Your preferred video source (Webcam, USB Camera, or RTSP Stream).

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security
- **Local Processing**: All video stays on your local machine. No video is uploaded to the cloud.
- **Telemetry Only**: Only anonymized movement data (coordinates) and match events are sent to the national hub.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting
- **Performance**: Ensure your device has at least 8GB of RAM and a dedicated GPU (optional but recommended) for best tracking performance.
- **Connection**: Check your internet connection if the status indicator remains "Disconnected".
